Starburst mascot

A square core with four blocky points, faceted like cut glass and extruded into violet depth. Eight voxel shards orbit it — they can be animated independently.

Full mark

Mascot + particles. Use at 48 px and above; below that, drop the particles.

04 · Usage

Keep the blocks breathing

Clear space

Keep at least the width of one star point (≈ ⅛ of the mark) clear on every side.

Minimum size

Full mark 48 px · icon 16 px · lockup 120 px wide. Below that, use the icon alone.

Backgrounds

Lime, white, and deep space indigo. Avoid mid-tone colors that fight the facets.

Don't

Recolor the facets, flatten the extrusion, stretch the wordmark, or add new effects.

App tile

Rounded-square 1024 px tile for macOS, Windows and IDE marketplace listings. The mascot keeps a 70% safe area and the face stays clear of the corner radius.
